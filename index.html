MISSION BRIEFING

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before boarding your spacecraft.

Passengers must be between 18–75 years of age, weigh between 50–110 kg, and pass a Zenith Medical Clearance Assessment. Most healthy individuals qualify. No prior spaceflight or aviation experience required.

Suborbital missions require a 3-day training programme. Orbital missions require a comprehensive 5-day programme at the Zenith Space Academy including zero-G simulation, emergency egress, suit operations, and spacecraft familiarisation.

Zenith maintains a 100% mission success rate across 12 completed flights and 247 passengers. Our spacecraft undergo over 2,000 hours of ground testing between missions. Every system has triple redundancy and autonomous abort capability at any flight phase.

Scrubbed launches are rescheduled within 30 days at no cost to the passenger. All accommodation and transfer costs during the standby period are covered by Zenith. We never launch unless every system is fully nominal.

Each passenger is allocated 500g of personal payload. Common items include family photographs, small mementos, and USB drives. All items must pass Zenith payload certification. Cameras and biometric wearables are fully approved.
